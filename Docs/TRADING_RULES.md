# XAUUSD Day Trading Rules

## Session & Timing
- **Session**: London/US Session
- **Timezone**: WIB (GMT+7)
- **Analysis Time**: 13:00-17:00 WIB (siang-sore)
- **Entry Time**: 19:00-23:00 WIB (malam)
- **Timeframe**: 5-minute chart

## Position Sizing
- **Risk per Trade**: 20%
- **Stop Loss**: Defined by support/resistance
- **Take Profit**: 1:1 to 1:2 risk-reward ratio

## Entry Rules
- Support/resistance breakout
- Volume confirmation
- Volatility threshold (ATR > X pips)

## Exit Rules
- Hit stop loss
- Hit take profit
- Time-based exit (4-hour hold max)
- News event before entry window

## Instrument
- **Symbol**: XAUUSD (Gold/USD)
- **Platform**: MetaTrader 5 (MQL5)

## Backtesting Requirements
- Minimum 100 trades per strategy
- Win rate > 45%
- Profit factor > 1.5
- Max drawdown < 30%
