# Trading Rules & Guidelines - XAUUSD Day Trading

**Effective Date**: 2026-05-10  
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-10

## 📋 Pre-Trade Checklist

Before entering ANY trade:

- [ ] Analysis done in afternoon/evening WIB
- [ ] Entry time is during London/US session (evening WIB)
- [ ] No major news events in next 1 hour
- [ ] Risk per trade = 20% of account
- [ ] Stop loss level identified (ATR × 1.5)
- [ ] Take profit levels calculated
- [ ] Risk/Reward ratio ≥ 1:1.5

## 🔄 Entry Rules

### Bollinger Bands Setup
- [ ] Price touches lower band = potential long
- [ ] Price touches upper band = potential short
- [ ] OR Bollinger Squeeze detected (bands compressed)
- [ ] Squeeze breakout = direction of next move

### ATR Confirmation
- [ ] ATR > 20-period average (adequate volatility)
- [ ] Stop loss = Current Price ± (ATR × 1.5)
- [ ] Don't trade when ATR is too low (choppy market)

### Volume & Price Action
- [ ] Volume above 20-period average
- [ ] Price near identified support/resistance
- [ ] Candle formation confirms direction

### Timing
- [ ] Entry between 18:00-23:00 WIB (evening)
- [ ] Avoid trading right at market open (high volatility)

## 📊 Exit Rules

### Take Profit Levels

| Level | Target | Exit Rule |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **TP1** | Risk × 1.5 | Take 50% profit |
| **TP2** | Risk × 2.5 | Take remaining 50% |
| **SL** | Risk × 1.0 | Cut loss immediately |

**Example:**
- Entry: 2410.50
- Stop Loss: 2408.00 (risk = 2.50 points = 20%)
- TP1: 2413.75 (2410.50 + 2.50×1.5) = 50% profit
- TP2: 2416.75 (2410.50 + 2.50×2.5) = full profit

### Time-Based Exit

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Trade open 4 hours | Close at market |
| 1 hour before session close | Close at market |
| Next day morning | Close at open |

### Trailing Stop (Optional)

Once TP1 hit and 50% closed:
- Move stop loss to breakeven
- Let remaining 50% run with trailing stop (20-30 pips)

## ⚠️ Risk Management Rules

### Maximum Risk Per Day

Daily Loss Limit = Risk × 3 trades
= 20% × 3
= 60% maximum daily loss

⚠️ **If daily loss limit reached: STOP TRADING FOR THE DAY**

### Position Sizing

Position Size = (Account Size × 20%) / Stop Loss Points
Example:

Account: $10,000
Risk: 20% = $2,000
Stop Loss: 2.50 points
Position Size = $2,000 / 2.50 = 800 units (0.8 lot)


### Forbidden Practices

- ❌ Averaging down (adding to losing positions)
- ❌ Revenge trading (over-trading after loss)
- ❌ Trading against major news
- ❌ Trading with gut feeling (only follow rules)
- ❌ Moving stop loss against you
- ❌ Changing plan mid-trade

## 📈 Trade Management Rules

| Stage | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Entry** | Place pending order 5 pips below/above key level |
| **Entry Confirmation** | Candle closes in favor of trade |
| **50% TP Hit** | Close 50% position, move SL to breakeven |
| **SL Hit** | Accept loss, move on to next opportunity |
| **Time Exit** | Close remaining at market |
