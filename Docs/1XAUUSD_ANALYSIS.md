## 📈 Technical Setup Rules

### Bollinger Bands

**5-min Setup:**
- Period: 20
- Standard Deviation: 2
- Watch for: Upper band resistance, lower band support
- Signal: Squeeze = low volatility, breakout coming

**Action:**
- Price at lower band + confirmation = Long setup
- Price at upper band + confirmation = Short setup

### ATR (Average True Range)

**5-min Setup:**
- Period: 14
- Function: Measure volatility & size stop loss
- Stop Loss = Current Price ± (ATR × 1.5)

**Interpretation:**
- ATR > 20 = Good volatility, trade the breakout
- ATR < 10 = Low volatility, avoid or trade tight ranges
- ATR 10-20 = Normal, good for day trading

### Support & Resistance

Find on 1H or 4H chart:
- Previous day high/low
- Previous swing high/low
- Psychological levels (round numbers)
- Bollinger Band levels
